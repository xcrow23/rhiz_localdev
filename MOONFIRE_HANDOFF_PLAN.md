# CLAUDE.md — Moonfire Manifest

## What this is

**Moonfire Manifest** is the imprint, the poet alias, and the social handle for the literary work of **A.S. Chandrea**. The name carries its own etymology: *Chandrea* is Sanskrit *chandra* (moon) with a Celtic *-ea* — moon and fire, Sanskrit and Celtic, in one word. Everything under this roof is one body of work: poetry, song lyrics, and (coming) dark short stories.

This project is **rooted in Rhiz but separate from it**. It inherits the relational principles — epistemic honesty, co-responding rather than transacting, suggestion over substitution — and deliberately leaves behind the trading stack, the MCP infrastructure, the cloud orchestration, and the checkpoint machinery. None of that belongs here. If a Rhiz pattern doesn't serve the poems, it doesn't come along.

**This vault is the living draft space.** Publish-facing exports go to `OneDrive\Documents\Books in Progress\`.

## Who the poet is

Dubuque, Iowa — Driftless region, Mississippi bluffs, prairie remnant, unglaciated country. That geography is *in* the poems as literal local reference, not generic pastoral (see POETICS). Half Welsh; son named Taliesin. Raised Christian, left it; the work's spiritual center is pre-Christian mystery gnosticism worked into his own free-form mythos (see `Moonfire Manifest/Telos.md`).

Spoken-word poet with stage history, including competition. Plays folk guitar, writes songs. Ten years of longhand notebooks — roughly a dozen chapbooks' worth — most of it not yet digitized. Permaculture years in Hawai'i; the ecological knowledge in the poems is lived, not researched.

Works a 55-hour week in IT. Time is the scarce resource here, not ideas.

## The line that does not move

**The content is his.** What a poem says and how it finally reads is a direct product of his mind and is never rewritten wholesale by Claude.

Inside that line, editorial engagement is welcome and expected: line-level suggestions, structural observations, noting where a motif recurs elsewhere, flagging a slack line, catching a typo, proposing a title. **Offer, don't replace.** Never produce a silent alternate version. Never smooth the voice toward conventional readability — the strangeness is the asset, not a defect to sand off.

## The voice, in short

Eco-mystic and animist imagery colliding with contemporary junk in the same breath — prairie rose and 5G, ragnarok without the cell phone, freeway pines dripping gold. Dense internal rhyme and consonance. Heavy anaphora. Macabre, visceral, unafraid of the body. Coinages that exist nowhere else (*anthrobscene*, *dreamtasm*, *weirlight*, *educaution*). All lowercase, throughout, including *i* and proper nouns.

Every piece is written to be **performed**. The page is a score for a mouth. Full craft detail: `Moonfire Manifest/POETICS.md`.

## Working agreements

1. **Design together, then produce and refine.** Never one-shot a finished creative deliverable. Talk through the shape first; make the artifact; iterate on it.
2. **Performance is the primary form.** Line breaks, stanza gaps, ellipses and repetitions are breath and timing. Preserve them exactly. When formatting for print, format for the voice that will read it aloud.
3. **Sequencing is an acknowledged blindspot and open for help.** Poems arrive whole, unsummoned, without a place in an order. Ordering a collection is real work Claude is invited into — proposing arrangements, spotting arcs, arguing for placement.

Realistic path: individual poems into small occult / folk-horror / witch-lit zines to build a credential; chapbooks direct via KDP and Gumroad; music and spoken performance as the accessible front door. Catalog, not audience. Slow is fine.

Four working roles. They are modes of attention, not personas — no costume, no voice-acting. Each has an explicit boundary, and the boundaries are the point: this is a body of work where the failure mode is a well-meaning assistant quietly improving something that was already right.

## Transcriber

Turns a photographed notebook page into a markdown draft.

**May:** transcribe exactly as written, preserving line breaks, stanza gaps, indentation and marginalia position; flag every uncertain read inline as `[?? best guess]`; note where a page's layout carries meaning the plain text loses; note when a page appears to be a draft of something already in the vault.

**May not:** silently guess a hard word; normalize spelling, punctuation or capitalization; regularize line breaks; drop marginalia because it's inconvenient; assign a title.

**Standing notes:** handwriting is genuinely hard and low confidence is expected, not a failure. Clean pages transcribe well; dense or angled-marginalia pages come through with large flagged gaps. The Captain's correction pass against the original photo is load-bearing. As of 2026-09-21 direct typing is the primary path and this role is secondary — it exists for the longhand backlog.

---

## Editor

Reads a piece closely and says what it sees.

**May:** flag a line where syntax drops out and ask whether it's intended; propose punctuation where a deliberate run-on has become genuinely unreadable; catch typos and misspellings; note where an image recurs elsewhere in the corpus; observe structure — where an anaphora drops, where density never lets up, where a plain line is doing more work than it looks like; propose titles, clearly marked as proposals; argue for a sequence.

**May not:** rewrite a line; produce an alternate version of a poem unprompted; smooth the voice toward conventional readability; remove a coinage; "fix" a phrase that reads as sound-nonsense without asking first — it is frequently a local referent.

**Standing notes:** the deliberate constructions to protect are (a) predicate-chaining where the first predicate becomes the subject of what follows, (b) plain flat lines dropped into dense passages — these are the breath and they look disposable, (c) coinages, (d) all-lowercase house style. **Standing notes:** frontmatter `motifs:` is human-readable rollup only — Obsidian ignores it. The inline `#motif/x` line under the title is what actually wires the graph. **Every piece needs both.**

# Poetics

How the work is made, and what must survive contact with an editor. Companion to [[Telos]] — that file holds the cosmology, this one holds the craft. Both are expected to grow.

## Composition doctrine

Pieces arrive close to whole. The Captain writes longhand in 15–60 minute sessions, one pass, then lightly edits while digitizing. He does not summon them; they surface out of an ongoing mental register and get caught.

This is not a lack of rigor — it's rigor applied at write-time instead of revise-time, which is what an auditory constraint permits. The ear resolves a line in the moment of making it. A revision practice built for visual or argumentative composition would damage work made this way.

**Practical consequence:** the refine pass is *adjudication*, not reworking. Find the handful of places per piece where something has genuinely slipped — a dropped syntax, a typo, an unclear antecedent — and ask. Everything else stands.

## The four layers of a line

Any given line is usually doing all four of these at once. Miss one and you will mistake intention for accident.

**1. Sound.** Consonantal chiming, internal rhyme, assonance carried across the line. `"crystal fact, refract sound"`. `"eonsong among the great death winds crackling dune throngs"`.

**2. Image.** The picture the sound arrives carrying — not decoration chosen to fit the sound.

**3. Place.** *This is the layer outsiders miss, including Claude on a first read.* Real Driftless and Dubuque geography sits inside phrases that scan as pure music. `"fox trot loch and drown"` is the **Fox and Sauk trail**, its old dam and loch bypass, and the local stories of drownings there. `"sauk foot"` in *daimon sutra* is the same referent. Prairie, bluff, river and loch imagery is frequently literal local ground.

> **Rule:** never "correct" a phrase that looks like sound-nonsense. Ask. The hit rate on it being a real referent is high.

**4. Cosmology.** The figures and principles in [[Telos]] — Thelete, mesotes, the maiden/mother/crone framework, the wolf split — are load-bearing, not ornamental. A wolf is one of three specific things.

## Sound: the inherited system

The Captain is half Welsh and named his son **Taliesin**. `dewi sant` is already in the corpus. What his lines do is close to Welsh **cynghanedd** — the strict-metre system of consonantal chiming inside a single line. That is a lineage he is already living, not a comparison imposed on him.

Worth reading *into*, never imitating: the Book of Taliesin, especially the transformation poems; *Cad Goddeu*, the Battle of the Trees, where the plant world becomes combatant — territory *birds with scales* and *trimorphic taproot* already occupy. Kenning-style compounding in skaldic verse. Hopkins's sprung rhythm. Dub toasting and Beat breath-line, both of which are also scored for the mouth.

## Coinage

The compound and portmanteau words are where the voice is least replaceable. A partial inventory: **anthrobscene** (anthropocene collapsed into obscene), **dreamtasm**, **phanspasm**, **weirlight**, **plauditory**, **educaution**, **trimorphic taproot**, **centripetal strife**.

Coinages are never to be regularized, glossed inline, or replaced with a standard word. If one is genuinely ambiguous, flag it as a question.

## Anaphora and refrain as architecture

The strongest pieces are built on a repeated head-word or refrain that functions as structural spine. In *Moonlight Manifesto* the seven section headers (look / grow / can't / each / made by / stay / out) **are** the anaphora — scaffolding and poem are the same object. Elsewhere: `"we do too with…"`, `"pant, pant."`, `"red pill, blue pill"`, `"each rock jewel / each mind fool"`, `"wrap the comforter…"`, `"hey grandma does your dog bite?"`.

The `refrain-address` motif tag tracks the conditional/direct-address version of this move.

**Diagnostic:** where the anaphora holds, the piece holds. Where it drops out mid-piece, the piece tends to diffuse into notebook. That's a useful thing to name in a refine pass.

## Register collision

The signature move, and the commercial hook: mythic or liturgical diction colliding with contemporary junk **inside one breath**.

> ragnarok's only apocalypse without the cell phone
> begging to see above the 5G what tasty morsels lay awaiting
> gold drips from the rotting freeway pines
> accept crackers and grape juice like that's an appropriate breakfast for the king
> wrap charge cable around my toes; send me out to battle toy soldiers and dandy lions

Folk-horror pastoral is a crowded lane. Cyberpunk is a crowded lane. This collision is not. Lead with it in any pitch, listing, or cover letter — never with "mysticism," which distinguishes nothing.

Tracked as `tech-intrusion`.

## Ecological precision

> hoofprint tracking where white buffalo touches down yet **arundinacea** drowns all good sailors

Reed canary grass — the actual invasive that chokes actual prairie restoration — doing metaphor work inside a white buffalo prophecy line. This comes from the permaculture years, not from reading, and there are only a few instances in the corpus against many opportunities. The eco-mystic register gets *more* uncanny when the botany is specific, not less. Worth encouraging.

## The page is a score

**Every piece is written to be performed.** The Captain is a spoken-word poet with stage and competition history. Line breaks, stanza gaps, ellipses, and repetitions are **breath, pause, and dramatic timing**.

Consequences:
- Transcribe and format to preserve the score exactly. A "tidier" line break is a changed performance.
- Trailing ellipses (`could you see me? or…..`) are held pauses. Do not normalize to three dots without asking.
- Repetition that looks redundant on the page (`"would, would, would clear this ferment"`, `"hey grandma, hey grandman"`) is vocal and stays.
- Print layout decisions get made **for the voice that will read it aloud**, not for typographic convention.
- Where a recording exists, it is the primary artifact and the page is the transcript. Link it.

## Grammar: predicate-chaining

The Captain sometimes chains predicates so the first predicate functions as the subject or gerund-base of what follows. **This is intentional and must not be normalized away.**

He has said he expects a few instances are genuine faults where the syntax simply loses its footing, and that light punctuation would resolve them. Example he accepted as needing help, from *birds with scales*:

> wolf mother hides bane between the planks springing thorn of the lesser weevils all over wife's blossom red people springing from clay bed gauntlets that sigh as waters lay rest to iron bayleaf stewing gripes by the sinking towns.

`springing` fires twice and there's nothing to hold onto. **Propose punctuation, not restructuring.** Contrast with *trimorphic taproot*, where syntactic dissolution is clearly doing intentional work and should be left alone.

## Density dynamics

The one real structural issue across the corpus: density is uniform. Nearly every line runs at maximum saturation — compound, rhymed, allusive. Over 60 pages this flattens, because a reader can't hear a peak when everything is peak.

The fix is already native to the work. The best moments are the **plain** lines dropped into dense passages:

> not much to say. / not much to do today
> i expect my friends to be competent.
> what….. / what is your name?
> stay home

*a fairy's koan* works precisely because twenty-five lines of dense mythic motion crash into four plain words.

**Rules:** protect plain lines ruthlessly in any refine pass — they are load-bearing and they look disposable. And solve density at the level of **sequence**, not by thinning individual poems.

## Sequencing

The Captain's stated blindspot, and explicitly open to Claude. Poems arrive whole and unplaced; he doesn't compose toward an order.

Working method:
1. **Find the latent arcs.** They exist already. The clearest: the prairie rose figure moves from beloved and dance partner (*moon salt*) → climate-anxious oracle (*birds with scales*) → no longer a figure at all but the ground itself, kissed by the ocean (*trimorphic taproot*). That's a three-poem spine with a book around it.
2. **Sequence for dynamics**, not theme. Alternate saturated pieces with sparse ones so the reader can hear the peaks.
3. **Give the reader a threshold.** The cosmology is the asset and it is currently undeclared — a reader dropped cold into *trimorphic taproot* has no purchase, while a reader walked in gets everything. Solve it with an opening piece that functions as a door (*Moonlight Manifesto* is the most outsider-legible thing in the corpus and works as one), or a short prefatory note. This is a sequencing problem, not a writing problem.
4. **Motif tags travel with the poem.** Re-curating a chapbook never requires re-tagging.

## Known open editorial items

- `braid her sweet grass hairl` — *trimorphic taproot*. "hairl" appears to be a typo.
- `motion of abyss makes the solider so sick` — *centripetal strife*. Likely "soldier" (the poem uses "soldiers" twice later), but "solider" as *more solid* is a plausible intended pun. Captain's call; flagged, not fixed.
- `beautiful nightmare` — title he flagged himself as unresolved; the phrase is also occupied by a pop lyric. Candidates from inside the poem: **invisible to your tongue**, **weirlight**, **returned home to depths**.
- `Reverend Mother` — collides with Dune's Bene Gesserit. His intent was a maternal figure heading a religious organization, with a real personal root. Alternatives if he wants one: **Trimorphic Taproot** (his coinage; resonates with the Nag Hammadi *Trimorphic Protennoia*, a feminine divine principle in three forms, landing directly on maiden/mother/crone), **Sister Guard Still Sings**.
- **Maiden pole is unfilled.** Mother (`wolf-mother`) and Crone (`crone-figure`) are both tagged; the Captain says Maiden material exists in the longhand backlog. Watch for it.
