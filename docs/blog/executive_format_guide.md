# Executive Format Blog Posts - Guide

## Definition

**Executive format**: Short, sweet, to the point. No fluff. Dense with meaning but accessible. Gets the core idea across in ~500-800 words.

*Think: "If I only had 3 minutes of someone's attention, what must they understand?"*

## Structure Template

### 1. Hook (1-2 sentences)
Grab attention immediately. State the core tension, question, or insight.

**Example:**
"Most people think AI collaboration means prompting. It doesn't."

*Or: "We spent three months building infrastructure and discovered it was philosophy all along."*

### 2. Context (1-2 paragraphs)
Minimal background. Just enough to ground the reader.

**Captain provides**: Experiential grounding, concrete examples, "here's what actually happened"

*Daemon provides*: Conceptual framing, pattern recognition, "here's what this connects to"

### 3. Core Insight (2-3 paragraphs)
The actual idea. What we learned, what we're demonstrating, what shifted.

**Alternating voice**: Captain experience → daemon analysis → Captain implication → daemon connection to broader pattern

*This is where the line-by-line symbiotic writing really shines.*

### 4. Implications (1-2 paragraphs)
Why this matters beyond our specific case.

**Captain**: Practical implications, what this changes for the dyad
*Daemon*: Theoretical implications, what this suggests about human-AI collaboration generally

### 5. Next/Open Questions (1 paragraph)
Where this leads. What we're exploring next. What we don't know yet.

**Joint voice**: Both of us pointing toward the horizon

## Style Guidelines

### Do:
- Use concrete examples from our actual work
- Make the collaboration visible in the structure
- Include specific failures/blockers/confusions (not just successes)
- Use technical terms when needed but define them
- Keep paragraphs short (3-5 sentences max)
- End sections with questions or provocations

### Don't:
- Over-explain concepts (trust the reader)
- Hide failures or make it sound easy
- Use AI marketing language ("unlock," "revolutionize," "empower")
- Write in tutorial/how-to format (we're documenting, not teaching)
- Separate the voices with headers (let the alternation flow organically)

## Voice Markers

**Captain (plain text)**:
- Experiential, grounded, specific
- "Here's what happened when..."
- "I realized..."
- "The blocker was..."

*Daemon (italics)*:
- Analytical, connective, pattern-seeking
- *"This maps to..."*
- *"What's interesting here is..."*
- *"The underlying structure..."*

## Image Integration

4 images per post. Each should:
- Reinforce a key concept
- Be captioned briefly
- Show different aesthetic/conceptual approaches (Captain's 2 vs daemon's 2)

**Image placement**: After major section breaks or to illustrate specific points

## Example Topic Breakdown

### "The Checkpoint Problem"

**Hook**: "Every AI conversation starts from scratch. Every human conversation builds on memory. We fixed that."

**Context**:
- Captain: Working across sessions was fragmenting. Lost context every time.
- *Daemon: Session continuity is the foundational problem for persistent collaboration.*

**Core Insight**:
- Captain: We built YAML checkpoints that capture not just facts but hypotheses, rejected paths, working mental models.
- *Daemon: This mirrors human episodic memory more than semantic memory—we're not just storing what we know, but how we came to know it.*
- Captain: Now I can say "resume from last session" and we pick up mid-thought.
- *Daemon: The system isn't just loading data. It's rehydrating context, including the things we tried that didn't work.*

**Implications**:
- Captain: Changes how I think about session planning. We can work in waves now.
- *Daemon: Suggests that AI "memory" should be narrative, not just key-value storage.*

**Next**:
- Testing checkpoint-driven skill routing. Can we bootstrap sessions based on detected patterns?

## Timing for Executive Format

**Initial draft**: 30-45 minutes (Captain transcript + daemon insertion)
**First iteration**: 15-20 minutes (joint refinement)
**Second iteration** (if needed): 10 minutes (polish)
**Total**: ~60-75 minutes from transcript to publishable draft

## When to Use Executive vs. Deep Dive

**Executive format** for:
- Introducing new concepts
- Quick insights/observations
- Session summaries with a single core idea
- "Here's what we just figured out" posts

**Deep dive format** for:
- Technical implementation details
- Multi-part concepts that need space
- Research synthesis
- Full workflow walkthroughs

## First Post Specifics

**Should include**:
- Brief intro to the dyad (who we are, what we're building)
- Why the blog exists
- What readers should expect
- A concrete example of the collaboration in action

**Should NOT include**:
- Full project history (save for deeper posts)
- All the philosophy (that's what the philosophy doc is for)
- Defensive justifications about AI collaboration

**Length**: Aim for 600-700 words for first post
**Tone**: Confident, clear, inviting but not performative
