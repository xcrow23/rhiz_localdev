
**look**

i don't know how to be with family because

they don't understand my zero ownership policy.

look, i'm holding broken toys;

singing to the cows who charm saturn and return my inner child,

look, i can catch my tears;

drown the flies in ribbons and triangles insuperably indifferent.

look, i'm ripping my pants; patching my eyes; hoping

you'll frisk my shirt too.



**grow**

points of view grow tired on my tongue.

opinions slip through my fingers asking why I drink from the wild stream.

agreement leaves me gasping; discord, strong. reminding how long

we've torn breathlessly from the looming threat of silence like

ragnarok's only apocalypse without the cell phone.

leave me in silence; gathering the stars only I can see.

leave me in dark; pointing you to all the lights.

leave me in between;

i'll show your optic nerve each and every glory only I have ever seen.



**can't**

i can't accept your savior because i do not believe beneficent omnipotence violates free will.

i can't wear that mask because i believe replicated consensus remains the only respectable culmination

of the scientific method.

i can't receive your emergency call because i do not believe tabula rasa leaves us defenseless.

i can't be your light because i believe different but equal leaves us all openly hiding our superpowers.

i can't make your date because i'm melting beeswax flakes to soften our hearts and

fill the classroom slate.



**each**

each person trammels through their own unique, socio-individual, no replication possible,

anthropomorphism of a life.

hence why I can reach my loving hands prayer pose over every dinner table, smoothing curtains,

mending sores, leave notes extolling your virtues to end up drunk;

laughing all the way to your place;

just to slap you, friend, in your disrespectful face.

because we measure, we portend brakeless grace.

because we see, we tend infinite sonder.



**made by**

your parents made you wear that costume because of me; burning ganja, smiles, crow's feet.

they bedecked you in customs so you wouldn't see our red party lights flash, no strife. look at all these

pretty girls, wiling, living their best life.

they had you kneel; accept crackers and grape juice like that's an appropriate breakfast for the king so

you wouldn't hear our musical laughter down the street, in the forest between the park and graveyard.

your parents put you to sleep in the dark just to keep you quiet during the day.

if i knock, would you toss your hair and run; smashing pumpkins all the way?



**stay**

stay home

moon set

sun grew rose

stay home

breath holds

rose copse blooms

stay home

tea bitter but moot

time fetters but roots

stay home

bedsheet blew

veil loose

stay home

i'll be crafting homunculi to live every dream we've spoken to rising winds so shy.

i'll be crafting homunculi so we can ask the anthropos every longing why.



**out**

sell me your creed,

i've been out carrying water, chopping wood.

sell me the death, you should.

i've been out speaking poems to rusted corner drip graffiti and tunes.

i've been out peein' on nature, spliffin', bending spoons and suffices.

sell me your needs,

i've been out wanting you clasped monogamous heart desire.

i've been out wanting to throw our complaints hearth fire, mix anthrobscene soup sweeter,

making love in our hybrid beater.

sell me your faithful cordae,

i've been out grasping your secret wishes like flicker strong spirits; playing with fuses and street lights.

i've been out of hope yet hoping you'll take my fingers, sweat, leave the bone; honey forked spoon

crook and i'll drive you home.
